me.txt

The open standard for human likeness rights

Like robots.txt tells crawlers what they can index, me.txt tells AI systems what they can do with your face. Register once. Enforce everywhere.

Why me.txt?

😶

Your face is being used

AI models train on billions of images scraped from the web. Deepfakes, synthetic ads, and AI-generated merch use real faces without consent. There is no opt-out mechanism — until now.

📜

A machine-readable consent file

me.txt is a plain-text file that declares who you are, what likeness data is registered, and exactly what uses you allow or deny — readable by any system, enforceable by anyone.

🛡

Evidence-backed enforcement

When someone uses your likeness without permission, me.txt generates timestamped evidence packets with match confidence, source URLs, and consent status — ready for takedown requests.

How it works

1

Upload your photos

Provide one or more photos of yourself. The system extracts facial embeddings — mathematical representations of your face — and stores them securely.

2

Set your consent rules

Choose exactly what uses you allow: editorial, commercial, AI training, satire. Deny everything by default, then allow only what you approve.

3

Get your me.txt

A permanent, public endpoint serves your consent file. Any AI system, platform, or rights-management tool can query it to check your permissions before using your likeness.
